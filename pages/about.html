Our Story

From a Single Truck to a Full Restoration Fleet

AquaRestore began in 2008 when founder James Harrison responded to a flooded neighborhood with nothing but a single extraction unit and a commitment to help. Word spread fast — not just because we worked hard, but because we genuinely cared.

Today, we operate a full fleet of restoration vehicles with a team of 40+ IICRC-certified technicians, serving hundreds of homeowners and businesses every year.
