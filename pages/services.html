Our Restoration Services

From immediate extraction to final structural repairs, we handle every phase of water damage recovery.

Emergency Water Extraction

Standing water is your property's worst enemy. It seeps into drywall, ruins flooring, and begins breeding mold within hours. Our rapid extraction process removes the bulk of the water immediately.

  • Submersible pump systems for deep water
  • Industrial wet vacuums for carpets and flooring
  • Immediate damage assessment
  • Safe disposal of contaminated water

Advanced Structural Drying

Once standing water is removed, materials look dry but remain wet inside. We use scientific drying methods to pull moisture out of the structural elements of your property.

Air Movers

High-velocity fans evaporate moisture from surfaces.

Dehumidifiers

Industrial units pull evaporated moisture from the air.

Moisture Mapping

Infrared technology to track hidden moisture pockets.

Progress Reports

Daily readings to monitor the drying progress.

Mold Prevention & Remediation

Mold can begin growing within 48 hours of water intrusion. Our mitigation strategy includes proactive antimicrobial treatments to prevent secondary damage and protect your indoor air quality.

  • Mold-Risk Assessment Identifying areas susceptible to fungal growth based on moisture levels.
  • Preventive Treatment Application of professional-grade antimicrobials to structural framing.
  • Air Quality Controls HEPA filtration and negative air machines to remove airborne spores.
